How the Adsterra impression calculator works

An impression is counted each time an ad is shown to a visitor. Adsterra pays publishers on a CPM basis for most formats, meaning a fixed amount per 1,000 impressions. The calculator uses the standard formula below.

Earnings = (Impressions ÷ 1000) × CPM × Your share

The estimated CPM starts from a base rate for your country and ad format. It is then adjusted for your traffic source and device split. The low and high values show a realistic spread (about 40% below to 70% above the average) because advertiser demand fluctuates.

Example

10,000 popunder impressions per day from the United Kingdom at a $3 CPM equals 10 × $3 = $30 per day, about $900 per month.

How to increase your Adsterra earnings

1. Focus on Tier-1 traffic

A visitor from the US, UK, Canada or Australia can be worth many times more than one from South Asia or Africa. Target content and promotion towards higher-paying regions.

2. Combine formats sensibly

Social Bar, Native Banner and a single popunder can coexist. Stacking too many intrusive units hurts retention and eventually lowers revenue.

3. Keep traffic clean

Bot, proxy and incentivised traffic is filtered and can get an account restricted. Quality traffic keeps CPM stable.

4. Optimise for mobile and desktop separately

Check which device performs better in your statistics and adjust placement and format accordingly.

5. Test and track

Use separate placements or sub-IDs to compare formats, then double down on the winner.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Adsterra pay per 1000 impressions?

It depends on country, ad format and traffic quality. Tier-1 countries such as the US, UK and Canada can reach roughly $2 to $6 CPM on popunder and direct link formats, while many Tier-3 countries earn under $0.50 CPM.

How is Adsterra revenue calculated?

Revenue = (impressions / 1000) x CPM. The CPM is the amount advertisers pay per 1000 impressions, and your final payout is the CPM Adsterra passes on to you after its share.

Which Adsterra ad format has the highest CPM?

Popunder and Direct Link usually have the highest CPM per impression. Social Bar, Native Banner and Display Banner earn less per impression but are less intrusive and can be stacked on the same page.

Why is my real Adsterra CPM lower than the estimate?

Real CPM changes daily with advertiser demand, seasonality, device mix, OS, browser and traffic source. Bot traffic, VPN traffic and low-quality sources also reduce rates sharply. Treat the calculator as a planning estimate, not a guarantee.
